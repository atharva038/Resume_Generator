# Pricing for Agents

Public read operations (templates catalog, guidelines, health check) are free. Paid actions require an authenticated account and user confirmation. All prices are in Indian Rupees (INR - ₹).

## Plans & Billing Details

### 1. Free Starter Tier
- **Price**: ₹0 (Free Forever)
- **Included Capabilities**:
  - 1 Active resume per month
  - 3 Core ATS templates (Classic, Modern, Minimal)
  - Standard OpenAI bullet optimization
  - Basic ATS keyword check & standard PDF export
  - 5 Career Q&A interview questions

### 2. One-Time Pass (Single Resume Boost)
- **Price**: ₹49 INR (Promotional sales as low as ₹9 / ₹19 / ₹29)
- **Duration**: 21-day full access per resume
- **Included Capabilities**:
  - 1 Dedicated resume with unlimited edits for 21 days
  - GPT-4o Enhanced AI rewriting & suggestions
  - Access to all 11+ ATS-certified templates
  - 1 Deep ATS diagnostic scan with gap analysis
  - 1 Live Public Portfolio website
  - Full Career Q&A Studio access & AI resume file parser

### 3. Pro Plan (Unlimited Career Accelerator)
- **Price**:
  - **Monthly**: ₹199 INR / month
  - **Yearly**: ₹1,990 INR / year (~17% discount, 2 months free)
- **Included Capabilities**:
  - Unlimited resumes & instant PDF exports
  - OpenAI GPT-4o AI engine
  - Access to all 11+ ATS-certified templates
  - Unlimited Deep ATS diagnostic scans & missing keyword analysis
  - Unlimited Public Portfolio websites
  - Unlimited Career Q&A mock interviews & AI voice coach
  - Unlimited AI resume file parsers (PDF/DOCX)
  - 2-Way Master Career Profile Hub sync & priority 24/7 support

## Machine-to-Machine Constraints
- Automated agents may retrieve template previews and public ATS guidelines without payment.
- Generation and export of Pro/Paid features require user authorization.
- Payments are processed securely via Razorpay with explicit interactive user confirmation.
